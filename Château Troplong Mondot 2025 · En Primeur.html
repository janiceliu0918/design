I  ·  The Estate
 
The summit of Saint-Émilion
 

A Premier Grand Cru Classé of Saint-Émilion, Château Troplong Mondot crowns the Mondot hill — at 110 metres, the highest point of the appellation. Its 33 hectares lie in a single block around the château, on a remarkable terroir of limestone and clay that lends the wine its purity, depth and unmistakable sense of place.

The 2025 Vintage — A hot, dry year ideally suited to this terroir, where limestone and clay kept a steady supply of water to the vines. Picked across an unusually long harvest — 28 August to 25 September — to capture each parcel at perfect ripeness, the 2025 is vibrant, intense and deeply aromatic, with fine, silky tannins and an endless finish.

 
 
 

From the limestone summit of Saint-Émilion — a wine of purity, depth and an endless, saline finish.
